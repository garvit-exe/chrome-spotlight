Chrome Spotlight Options

Configure your preferences for Chrome Spotlight.

To customize the keyboard shortcut:

  1. Go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  2. Find "Chrome Spotlight".
  3. Click the edit icon (pencil) next to "Open Chrome Spotlight" and press your desired key combination.

Recommended: Choose a combination that doesn't conflict with system shortcuts (like Cmd+Space on Mac or Alt+Space on Windows).
